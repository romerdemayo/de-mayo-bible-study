DE MAYO BIBLE STUDY APP — OFFLINE WEB

HOW TO OPEN ON MAC OR WINDOWS
1. Unzip the downloaded file.
2. Open the folder “De_Mayo_Bible_Study_App_v1”.
3. Double-click “index.html”.
4. It opens in Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox and works without internet.

IMPORTANT
- Keep index.html, app.js, styles.css, and bible-data.js together in the same folder.
- Your notes, favourites, prayers, plans, sermons, and lessons are saved in your browser on that device.
- Use Backup in the app before clearing browser data or moving computers.
- The included Bible text is the public-domain King James Version, sourced from Project Gutenberg eBook #10.

- Complete 31,102-verse WEB

- Full-text and reference search
