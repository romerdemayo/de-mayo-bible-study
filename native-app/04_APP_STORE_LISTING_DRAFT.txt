APP NAME
De Mayo Bible Studies

SUBTITLE
English & Tagalog Bible Study

PROMOTIONAL TEXT
Read, study, pray, and prepare with free English and Tagalog Bible resources for individuals, families, teachers, and ministries.

DESCRIPTION
De Mayo Bible Studies is a bilingual Christian study app designed to help people read Scripture, prepare lessons, build devotionals, pray with guidance, and share biblical teaching.

Features include:
- English and Tagalog Bible reading
- Bible studies and devotionals
- Exhortation preparation tools
- Kids Bible lessons
- Prayer library
- Reading plans and personal notes
- Offline access to key content

The app is provided as a ministry resource by De Mayo Bible Ministry.
