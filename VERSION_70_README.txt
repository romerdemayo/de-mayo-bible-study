Saved Sermon Presentation improvements:
- Saved sermons are collapsed by default.
- Tap the sermon heading to expand or collapse it.
- Each saved sermon has a Present button.
- Presentation View hides the creator, navigation, other sermons, editing controls, and footer.
- Only the selected sermon appears on the connected monitor.
- Presentation View attempts browser full-screen mode and includes an Exit Presentation button.
- Responsive reading sizes are included for phone, tablet, desktop, and large monitor displays.
