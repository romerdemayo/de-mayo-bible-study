DE MAYO BIBLE STUDIES — VERSION 68

Sermon Studio improvements:
- All sermon fields may be left blank.
- Create Sermon now selects a complete sermon topic at random.
- It avoids sermon titles already saved in the Sermon Library until all built-in sermon ideas have been used.
- The generated sermon includes title, passage, theme, purpose, introduction, context guide, three developed points, supporting Scriptures, applications, Gospel connection, questions, closing challenge, conclusion, prayer, and suggested reading.
- Users can edit the complete sermon before saving.
- Saving an empty sermon also generates a complete unused sermon automatically.
