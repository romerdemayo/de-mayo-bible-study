FREE IPHONE/IPAD INSTALLATION

1. Open Safari on the iPhone or iPad.
2. Visit https://romerdemayo.github.io/de-mayo-bible-study/
3. Tap the Share button.
4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
5. Tap Add.

The De Mayo Bible Studies icon will appear like an app. This method is free and does not require the Apple App Store.
